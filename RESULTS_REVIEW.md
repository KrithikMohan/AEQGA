# Pantheon+ and Kingston results review

The 300 saved Pantheon+ simulation runs give mean ± sample standard deviation
of 72.865 ± 0.186 for H0 and 0.35689 ± 0.01471 for Omega_m. These are
run-to-run optimizer spreads. The paper's Figure 4 reports 72.81 ± 0.22
and 0.362 ± 0.016. The configured local classical minimum is 72.83204,
0.362876; direct distances give 72.83735, 0.362369. The Omega_m mean
remains lower by 0.00548 using the direct reference, so the grid shift
alone does not account for the discrepancy. Keep the original estimates
and disclose the offset. The comparison does not identify its cause.

The Kingston archive contains one four-qubit single-circuit pilot and one
four-circuit generation. Each saved circuit contains 1,024 shots. Use
the generation as evidence of hardware execution. Equal best fits
after one update can result from carrying forward the same initial elite.

The historical analyses use reconstructed circuits. Their TVD and
population differences are exploratory until the original submitted
circuits can be authenticated. The verified comparison workflow requires
the archived logical circuits, fingerprints, and optimizer provenance.
Use it when those inputs exist. Keep the original checkpoints and plots
as historical artifacts.
